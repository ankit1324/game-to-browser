Move

          

            
←→

↑

Shift

Sword

          

            
←→
Advance or retreat

            
Shift
Strike

            
↑
Block

            
↓
Sheathe

          

        


        

          
Game

          

            
Esc
Pause

            
Space
Time left

            
Ctrl+A

Ctrl+G

F6
Quicksave

            
F9
Quickload

            
Ctrl+S

Backspace

Alt+Enter
Fullscreen
